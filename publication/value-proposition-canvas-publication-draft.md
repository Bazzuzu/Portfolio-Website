# Value Proposition Canvas

The Value Proposition Canvas is a Strategyzer tool for exploring how an offering addresses a specific customer segment’s needs. It connects a customer profile of jobs, pains, and gains with a value map of products and services, pain relievers, and gain creators.

## What are its six parts?

Customer jobs describe what people want to accomplish. Pains capture obstacles, costs, and undesirable outcomes. Gains describe the benefits they seek. The value map records what the team offers and how it intends to address those pains and gains.

## Example: Helping a hotel coordinate room turnover

A hotel manager’s job is to make rooms ready for arriving guests. A possible pain is learning about an unresolved repair after assigning a room. A desired gain is confidence about which rooms are ready.

A proposed operations tool offers a shared room-status view. A linked maintenance issue could relieve the late-discovery problem. A clear record of readiness could support the manager’s decision. These connections are hypotheses to investigate with hotel staff.

## How do you use the canvas?

Focus on one segment and prioritize its most consequential jobs, pains, and gains. Discuss the proposed connections with customers, observe the task, and test an early version of the offering. Record which assumptions the evidence supports and which need revision.

The canvas helps organize a value proposition. Decisions about pricing, delivery, and the wider business still need their own evidence and planning.
