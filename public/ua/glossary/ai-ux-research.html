UX-дослідження з ШІ (AI UX Research)

ШІ та агентні системи • UX та архітектура продукту

ШІ в UX-дослідженнях (AI in UX Research) — це застосування інструментів штучного інтелекту для підтримки дослідницьких активностей, таких як підготовка запитань, структурування спостережень або складання резюме. Згенеровані матеріали мають залишатися чітко відокремленими від емпіричних даних, зібраних під час взаємодії з реальними респондентами.

Where can AI support UX research?

A tool can propose interview questions, organize supplied notes, suggest candidate themes, or help locate relevant passages. Each use still needs a clear research question and a way to check the result.

Activity Useful support Required check
Preparation Draft questions or task wording Remove leading assumptions
Transcription Produce a searchable draft Verify important words and speaker attribution
Analysis Suggest clusters or exceptions Compare with the original observations
Reporting Draft a concise account Trace claims and quotes to evidence

An AI-generated persona or hypothetical answer is an assumption to investigate. It does not establish what a real participant thinks or does.

Example: checking a proposed research theme

Suppose a researcher supplies approved, de-identified notes about a quote-request form. A tool proposes a theme: “People avoid sharing a phone number.”

The researcher checks the underlying sessions. Some participants may object to calls; others may not understand why the field is required. Combining these into one explanation could lead to the wrong design response.

Keep the observations, interpretation, and proposed action separate. Preserve contradictory evidence and the boundaries of the study.

How do you preserve traceability?

Give observations stable references to sessions, passages, or timestamps. Ask for those references in generated summaries, then verify that they exist and support the claim.

Do not turn a paraphrase into a quotation. Check any quote against the original recording or transcript. Report sample counts only when the underlying records and counting rule support them.

GOV.UK's analysis guidance distinguishes observed evidence from its interpretation and resulting actions. AI support should preserve that distinction.

What about participant data?

Use an approved workflow for the actual data involved. Check consent, access, retention, and the tool's handling of uploaded material before submitting recordings or notes.

Removing a name does not necessarily remove identifying details from a story. Supply only what the task needs, and keep human review of consequential interpretations.

Evaluate the workflow by the quality and usefulness of the findings, including checking effort. A faster summary that loses important exceptions can make the research less useful.
