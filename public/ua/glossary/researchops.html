ResearchOps

UX та архітектура продукту

ResearchOps (операційне забезпечення досліджень) — це система координації спеціалістів, процесів, інструментів і ресурсів, яка забезпечує ефективне проведення досліджень у компанії. Вона допомагає командам рекрутувати учасників, адмініструвати дослідницькі артефакти, координувати інтерв'ю та тестування, а також робити аналітичні інсайти доступними для прийняття продуктових рішень.

What does ResearchOps cover?

The exact responsibilities vary with the organization and its research program.

Area Typical operational work
Participants Recruitment processes, scheduling, communication, and incentives
Consent and data Approved handling, access, retention, and participant requests
Tools Access, setup, evaluation, and support
Knowledge Findable evidence, research records, and clear source context
Coordination Shared calendars, support, and repeatable practices
Capability Onboarding and support for people conducting research

The ResearchOps Community describes a broad framework for supporting research across organizations. A small team can apply the relevant practices without creating a separate department.

Example: repeated recruitment delays

Suppose several product teams repeatedly struggle to recruit the same type of participant. ResearchOps can help establish a shared recruitment process, clear eligibility criteria, scheduling support, and a record of prior participation.

Researchers still decide which participants fit each question and which method is appropriate. A reusable process should not quietly narrow every study to the easiest people to reach.

Review who is missing from the recruitment approach, including people with relevant access needs.

ResearchOps vs. DesignOps vs. research

DesignOps supports the broader operation of design work. ResearchOps focuses on the conditions and infrastructure that make research possible and useful.

Conducting research includes defining questions, selecting methods, collecting evidence, and interpreting findings. Operational support enables that work; it does not replace the research judgment involved.

Responsibilities may overlap, and one person may perform several roles. Make ownership clear enough that a team knows where to get help.

What changes when research tools use AI?

Review what material the tool receives, how it is handled, and how generated interpretations remain traceable to evidence. Maintain a distinction between participant statements and AI-produced summaries.

An automatically generated research repository still needs appropriate access, source references, and maintenance. Searchable findings can be misleading if they omit the study's date, sample, context, or limitations.

Start with a recurring bottleneck and evaluate whether the operational change improves research quality and access as well as reducing coordination effort.
