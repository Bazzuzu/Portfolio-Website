Time-to-Value (TTV)

Product Strategy & Growth • UX & Product Architecture

Definition

Time-to-Value (TTV) measures the elapsed duration—in steps, clicks, or minutes—from a user's initial account creation to their first realization of tangible product value.

Purpose

Reduces early abandonment by eliminating friction, unnecessary setup steps, and cognitive hurdles during onboarding.

Key Concepts

Friction reductionprogressive onboardingimmediate utilitydeferred setup.

How It Works

Designers streamline first-run wizards, defer registration forms, provide interactive templates, and utilize sample data so users experience core value immediately.
