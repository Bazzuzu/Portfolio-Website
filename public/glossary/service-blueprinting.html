Service Blueprinting

UX & Product Architecture • Product Strategy & Growth

Definition

Service Blueprinting is an operational diagramming methodology that visualizes end-to-end service delivery processes, mapping customer touchpoints against frontstage employee actions, backstage operational workflows, and internal support systems.

Purpose

Uncovers cross-departmental bottlenecks, system failures, and hidden technical dependencies that disrupt customer-facing interactions.

Key Concepts

Line of visibilityfrontstage interactionsbackstage operationssupport processes.

How It Works

Cross-functional workflows are chronologically mapped on vertical swimlanes across the line of customer interaction, exposing technological triggers and operational friction.

Related Terms

  • Customer Journey Mapping
  • Operational UX
  • Information Architecture (IA)

Sources
