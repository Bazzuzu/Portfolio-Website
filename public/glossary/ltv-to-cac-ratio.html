LTV to CAC Ratio

Product Strategy & Growth • Product Strategy & Growth Established

Definition

The LTV to CAC Ratio is a financial unit economics metric comparing the total lifetime revenue value generated by a customer against the total cost incurred to acquire them.

Purpose

Evaluates the long-term commercial sustainability and marketing efficiency of a software product, directly steering product investment decisions.

Key Concepts

Unit economicspayback periodchurn mitigationcustomer acquisition spend.

How It Works

Calculated as Ratio = Lifetime Value (LTV) / Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC), where LTV = (Average Revenue Per User (ARPU) * Gross Margin) / Churn Rate. A healthy benchmark for SaaS is generally considered 3 : 1 or higher.
