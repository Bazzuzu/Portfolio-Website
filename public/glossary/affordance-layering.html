Affordance Layering

UX & Product Architecture • Design Systems & Engineering Established

Definition

Affordance Layering is the intentional sequencing of visual, physical, and cognitive interaction signifiers based on user proximity, pointer focus, or operational state.

Purpose

Eliminates visual noise in high-density enterprise software by displaying secondary and tertiary action controls only when contextually relevant.

Key Concepts

Perceived affordanceprogressive visibilityinteraction triggersfocus reveal.

How It Works

Primary affordances remain permanently visible; secondary tools appear on pointer hover, row selection, or focus transitions, reducing cognitive clutter.
