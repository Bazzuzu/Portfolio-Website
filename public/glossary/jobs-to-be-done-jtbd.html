Jobs to Be Done (JTBD)

Product Strategy & Growth • UX & Product Architecture Established

Definition

Jobs to Be Done (JTBD) is a strategic innovation framework asserting that customers do not buy products; they "hire" products to make progress within specific circumstances and accomplish functional, emotional, or social jobs.

Purpose

Focuses innovation on underlying human motivations and desired progress rather than shallow demographic attributes or surface feature requests.

Key Concepts

Core jobfunctional/emotional progresspushing/pulling forcesanxiety/habit friction.

How It Works

Researchers interview users to uncover the catalyst events, emotional anxieties, and progress goals that trigger switching from an old solution to a new product.
