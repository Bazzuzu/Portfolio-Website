Product Discovery

Product Strategy & Growth • UX & Product Architecture Established

Definition

Product Discovery is the continuous, collaborative process of conducting user research, prototyping, and testing to determine what to build, validating value, usability, feasibility, and business viability before engineering commits code.

Purpose

Prevents engineering teams from building features that users do not need, do not understand, or refuse to pay for, eliminating product waste.

Key Concepts

Dual-track agilecontinuous interviewsassumption testingprototype validation.

How It Works

Product triads (Design, Engineering, Product Management) continuously test business and user assumptions using quick experiments running parallel to delivery sprints.
