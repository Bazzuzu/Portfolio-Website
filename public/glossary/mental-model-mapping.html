Mental Model Mapping

UX & Product Architecture • Product Strategy & Growth Established

Definition

Mental Model Mapping is the research practice of systematically diagramming a user's internal cognitive beliefs, situational reasoning, and workflows relative to a real-world task, independent of technological constraints.

Purpose

Identifies systemic disconnects between how users believe a system functions and the underlying system mechanics, guiding intentional interaction architectures.

Key Concepts

Cognitive schematask space mappingconceptual gap analysisbehavioral patterns.

How It Works

Qualitative behavioral research is synthesized into affinity matrices showing user motivations, contrasted against existing technological touchpoints to discover missing capabilities.
