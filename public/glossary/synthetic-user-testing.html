Synthetic User Testing

AI & Agentic Systems • Product Strategy & Growth Emerging

Definition

Synthetic User Testing is the research simulation practice of executing automated user experience audits, heuristic reviews, and usability walk-throughs using AI agents prompted with precise behavioral personas.

Purpose

Accelerates discovery cycles and catches glaring usability defects early in sprint cycles before investing time in expensive human cohort testing.

Key Concepts

Persona promptingagentic simulationautomated heuristic evaluationssynthetic bias calibration.

How It Works

Personas are parameterized into agent harnesses that navigate digital prototypes, recording task completions, perceived friction points, and heuristic scores.
