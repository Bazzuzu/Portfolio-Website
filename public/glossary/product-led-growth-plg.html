Product-Led Growth (PLG)

Product Strategy & Growth • UX & Product Architecture Established

Definition

Product-Led Growth (PLG) is a business go-to-market strategy that relies on the product experience itself—such as free tiers, self-serve onboarding, and viral expansion loops—as the primary driver of acquisition, retention, and expansion.

Purpose

Reduces customer acquisition costs (CAC) and accelerates enterprise expansion by empowering end-users to experience immediate value before sales interactions.

Key Concepts

Self-serve onboardingfreemium mechanicsproduct viral loopsproduct qualified leads (PQL).

How It Works

Removes upfront paywalls and sales gates, engineering rapid time-to-value so that the user realizes organic utility and triggers expansion within their organization.
