B2B SaaS Interaction Model

Product Strategy & Growth • UX & Product Architecture Established

Definition

A B2B SaaS Interaction Model is the structural interaction framework specifically engineered for multi-tenant, role-governed cloud platforms handling complex enterprise data, workflows, and administrative controls.

Purpose

Balances high-density functional efficiency for daily power users with intuitive simplicity for executive buyers and occasional administrative reviewers.

Key Concepts

Multi-tenancyrole-based interfacesbulk operationsworkspace switching.

How It Works

Deploys adaptable screen architectures featuring collapsible rails, complex tabular data grids, contextual actions, and permission-aware view gating.
