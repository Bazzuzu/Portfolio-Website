Churn Mitigation Architecture

Product Strategy & Growth • UX & Product Architecture Established

Definition

Churn Mitigation Architecture is the deliberate system of product signals, automated health checks, offboarding flows, and value-recovery loops designed to retain at-risk customers.

Purpose

Protects recurring subscription revenue by identifying customer disengagement early and resolving dissatisfaction before account cancellation.

Key Concepts

Predictive health scoresoffboarding surveyspause/downgrade optionsexit feedback loops.

How It Works

Telemetry algorithms detect drops in user logins or feature usage, triggering contextual re-engagement interventions, self-serve account pauses, or targeted customer success outreach.
