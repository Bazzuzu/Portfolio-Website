Design Engineering

Design Systems & Engineering • UX & Product Architecture Established

Definition

Design Engineering is a specialized hybrid practice operating at the boundary of interaction design and frontend software engineering, building prototypes, design tooling, token pipelines, and UI runtimes.

Purpose

Eliminates fidelity loss between design intent and production implementation, accelerating product innovation through rapid, code-level interaction prototyping.

Key Concepts

Design-to-code pipelineshigh-fidelity code prototypingUI performancetoken toolchains.

How It Works

Design engineers construct custom plugins, maintain token build processes, build complex animations, and contribute directly to production component architectures.
