ORCA Framework

UX & Product Architecture • Product Strategy & Growth Proprietary / Framework-Specific

Definition

The ORCA Framework is an iterative four-phase implementation process for Object-Oriented UX, guiding design teams through the sequential definition of Objects, Relationships, Calls to Action (CTAs), and Attributes across Discovery, Requirements, Prioritization, and Representation rounds.

Purpose

Deconstructs complex enterprise requirements into unambiguous structural components, ensuring domain models directly inform navigation, relational integrity, and component logic.

Key Concepts

Discovery roundRequirements matrixCTA matricesObject mapping passes.

How It Works

Teams extract domain nouns, map bidirectional relationships, define state-dependent operations, and enumerate descriptive metadata across iterative passes.
