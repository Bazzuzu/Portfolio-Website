Маржинальний прибуток (Contribution Margin)

Продуктова стратегія та зростання

Маржинальний дохід (Contribution Margin) — це виторг за вирахуванням змінних витрат. Він показує, яка частка коштів залишається для покриття постійних витрат і, після їх покриття, для формування операційного прибутку. Показник може виражатися загальною сумою, сумою на одиницю продукту або відсотком від виторгу.

What are the contribution margin formulas?

  • Total contribution margin = total revenue − total variable costs.
  • Contribution per unit = revenue per unit − variable cost per unit.
  • Contribution margin ratio = contribution margin ÷ revenue × 100.

State the unit and time period. One order, one active account-month, and one completed task are different units.

Variable costs change with the relevant activity. Fixed costs remain unchanged within a stated activity range and period. A salaried support team, for example, does not necessarily cost less immediately because a workflow generates fewer tickets.

A SaaS contribution margin example

Consider one illustrative active account-month, with these documented cost classifications:

Item Amount Treatment in this example
Revenue $100 Revenue for the month.
Variable delivery costs $12 Included in cost of revenue.
Allocated fixed delivery costs $8 Included in cost of revenue, excluded from variable costs.
Recurring partner commission $5 Variable selling expense outside cost of revenue.

Contribution per account-month = $100 − $12 − $5 = $83.

Contribution margin ratio = $83 ÷ $100 × 100 = 83%.

At 100 equivalent account-months, total contribution is $8,300 before fixed costs. This is not $8,300 of net profit.

The $5 commission is an ongoing monthly expense in this example. If acquisition spending is evaluated elsewhere in the model, reconcile the cost pools to avoid subtracting the same commission twice.

Why can contribution margin differ from gross margin?

Using the same example, cost of revenue is $12 + $8 = $20. Gross profit is therefore $80 and gross margin is 80%.

Contribution is $83 because it excludes the $8 fixed allocation but includes the $5 variable commission. The higher percentage does not mean the business earned extra money. It means the two measures subtract different cost categories.

For internal decisions, list every included cost rather than relying on a label such as “margin after costs.” If you remove only selected variable costs, name that narrower subtotal clearly.

How does contribution margin inform product decisions?

Suppose a workflow consumes a paid third-party check on every submission. A clearer preview might help users correct mistakes before submitting, reducing unnecessary checks.

Measure successful submissions, repeated checks, and the variable cost per completed task. Include the implementation effort and any new fixed expenses in the broader decision.

With positive contribution, additional volume helps cover fixed costs under unchanged assumptions. With negative contribution, each additional unit increases the shortfall. That conclusion depends on prices, costs, and capacity remaining as modeled; growth may require new capacity.

What should designers avoid?

Do not treat every allocated cost as an immediate saving. Reducing support work can free capacity while payroll remains unchanged. Report the capacity benefit separately from cash savings.

Also check customer outcomes. Blocking legitimate retries or making support harder to reach can reduce a visible cost while leaving users unable to complete their work.
