Період окупності CAC

Продуктова стратегія та зростання

Період окупності CAC (CAC Payback Period) — це час, необхідний для повернення вартості залучення клієнта (CAC) за рахунок доходу, який він генерує. У SaaS-індустрії цей показник коригується на валову маржу, відстежує окупність через валовий прибуток і вимірюється в місяцях.

What is the CAC payback formula?

For a simplified subscription model:

CAC payback in months = CAC ÷ (average monthly revenue per account × gross margin percentage).

The denominator is monthly gross profit per account. Use the same customer definition and cost boundary as your CAC and LTV calculations.

This shortcut assumes the account continues paying and produces stable monthly gross profit. It does not model cancellations, a gradual increase in usage, or changes in delivery costs.

If monthly gross profit is zero or negative, there is no finite payback under the unchanged assumptions. A negative arithmetic result is not a negative recovery time.

A SaaS payback example

An illustrative business spends $100,000 to acquire 100 paying accounts. Each account generates $100 in monthly revenue with $20 in cost of revenue.

Calculation Result
CAC: $100,000 ÷ 100 $1,000
Monthly gross profit per account: $100 − $20 $80
Gross margin: $80 ÷ $100 80%
Simplified payback: $1,000 ÷ $80 12.5 months

With monthly contributions posted at month-end, the account crosses the $1,000 threshold in month 13 if it remains a customer.

Dividing $1,000 by $100 gives 10 months, but that revenue-only calculation leaves out delivery costs. Label the basis whenever comparing payback figures.

How do you account for churn and changing usage?

Track the acquired cohort as a group. For each month after acquisition, subtract that cohort's cost of revenue from its revenue. Add the monthly gross profit until the cumulative total reaches the cohort's original acquisition spending.

Keep accounts that cancel in the original cohort; their later contribution is zero. Averaging only surviving customers makes recovery appear faster.

In the example, 2% monthly customer churn means the cohort will not keep generating its initial $8,000 of gross profit each month. A model that includes those departures therefore gives a longer payback than the 12.5-month shortcut, assuming the other inputs stay unchanged.

For usage-based products, track changing consumption and costs directly. If AI usage increases revenue while also increasing delivery costs, additional revenue alone does not establish faster payback.

CAC payback vs. LTV:CAC

LTV:CAC compares estimated lifetime value with acquisition cost. Payback measures how quickly acquisition cost is recovered.

Two products can have the same lifetime ratio and different payback periods. If each has $1,000 CAC and $4,000 estimated gross-profit LTV, one might generate $80 monthly gross profit while another generates $40 over a longer customer relationship.

A favorable lifetime ratio can still leave a long period before acquisition spending is recovered.

What is a good CAC payback period?

There is no universal target. Customer segment, retention, margin, sales cycle, and available cash all affect interpretation.

Compare the same calculation across comparable cohorts. Check whether customers tend to leave before acquisition spending is recovered, and whether the business can fund the intervening costs.

Annual upfront billing changes cash collection timing. It does not make a year of gross profit earned immediately; distinguish cash recovery from the gross-profit payback measure used here.

How can product designers use payback?

Investigate delays between acquisition and paid value: incomplete setup, failed first tasks, or confusing upgrades. Measure whether a change helps appropriate accounts reach sustained paid use, with support costs and cancellations included.

An interface that pushes an unsuitable annual commitment might improve cash collection while increasing refunds or distrust. Assess the whole customer outcome before interpreting a shorter cash-recovery period as a product improvement.
